# Privacy Policy for Zzlog

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Airoun Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides Zzlog (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App or contact us.

## 1. Recordings and sleep records stored on your device

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in with us. We do not operate a server to receive or store your recordings or sleep records.

**Microphone.** When you start a sleep session, the App uses your device’s microphone to listen for sounds such as snoring while you sleep. Recording continues in the background, with an ongoing notification, until you finish the session. The microphone is used only during a session you start. You can manage microphone permission in your device settings; denying it prevents sleep recording from working.

**On-device processing.** Sound levels, the detection of sound moments, and the classification of those moments into types such as snoring, speech, coughing or sneezing, and pet sounds are all performed on your device. Audio is not sent to us or to any third party for this processing.

**What is stored.** The App stores the following locally on your device:

- Sleep session records, such as start and end times
- Sound levels summarized once per minute, used for the sound chart
- Short audio clips of detected sound moments, with their time, length, loudness, and sound type
- Your App settings, such as which sound types to keep, detection sensitivity, and clip retention period

The App does not keep a continuous recording of your whole night. Depending on your settings, it saves only short clips of detected moments, or no audio files at all.

**Other people’s sounds.** Recordings may capture sounds made by other people in the room, such as a partner. Please let them know if you use the App near them.

**Motion sensors.** During a session, the App reads your device’s motion sensors (gyroscope and accelerometer) to avoid counting sounds made while you pick up or move your phone. This sensor data is used only on your device at that moment and is not stored.

**Notifications.** The App uses notification permission to show the ongoing notification required for recording in the background.

**Backups.** Audio clips are excluded from Android backup and device transfer, so they are not copied to backups or to a new device. Sleep session records and settings may be copied by Android backup and device-transfer features according to your device settings and backup provider’s practices.

**Not a medical service.** Snoring counts and sound levels in the App are provided to help you reflect on your nights. The App does not diagnose sleep stages, sleep disorders, or any health condition.

## 2. Usage analytics and crash reports

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, which send usage and diagnostic information to Google. The App does not display advertisements, but using these services means that the App is not free of data collection. Your recordings, audio clips, and sleep records are not sent to these services.

### Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Analytics to understand App usage and improve the App. It processes information such as App interactions, sessions, automatically recorded purchase events, device and App information, and App-instance identifiers. IP addresses are used to derive approximate location. Android advertising identifiers may also be collected depending on device availability and settings. These identifiers distinguish App installations or devices; they are not the same as a named account with us.

See [Google’s Analytics data disclosure information](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11582702?hl=en).

### Firebase Crashlytics

We use Crashlytics to investigate crashes and improve reliability. It processes crash traces, diagnostic information, device and operating-system information, App versions, and installation identifiers. Analytics events may appear as diagnostic breadcrumbs in crash reports.

See [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 3. Purchases

Where a paid version of the App is available, Google Play processes the purchase. The App does not collect your payment-card details. Google handles payment and transaction records under its own policies. Deleting App data does not delete your Google Play purchase history.

## 4. Support emails

If you contact airoun.team@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you include, such as your name, message, and attachments. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and address the issue you report. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, recordings of other people, or unnecessary sensitive information.

We delete support correspondence and associated personal information without undue delay once the inquiry has been resolved and the information is no longer needed for that purpose. Where applicable law requires retention, we retain the required information for the legally required period.

## 5. Service providers and international processing

We use Google’s Analytics, Crashlytics, Google Play, and Gmail services for the purposes described above. Information processed by these services may be handled on servers outside your country, including in the United States. System-managed backups are handled by the backup provider you use.

Google’s handling of information is described in its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the applicable service terms. Google’s Firebase privacy information also describes its international data-transfer arrangements. We do not sell your personal information.

## 6. Retention

**Audio clips:** Clips are deleted automatically from your device after the retention period you choose in the App (7, 14, or 30 days; 30 days by default). If you turn off clip saving or deselect a sound type, new clips of that type are not kept; the related counts and sound chart remain.

**Sleep records and settings:** These remain on your device until you clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App. Uninstalling normally removes local App data, but system backups of sleep records and settings may remain and must be managed separately.

**Analytics:** Our event-data retention setting is 2 months, and our user-data retention setting is 14 months. Reset on new user activity is enabled, so new activity renews the applicable user-data retention period. These settings do not limit most standard aggregated reports. See [Google Analytics data retention](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196?hl=en).

**Crashlytics:** Google retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning deletion from live and backup systems, as described in its [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

**Support correspondence:** We follow the deletion rule in Section 4.

**Google Play records and system backups:** These are subject to the relevant provider’s retention practices and your account settings, rather than the App’s local deletion controls.

## 7. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose which sound types to keep as clips, turn off clip saving, and set how long clips are kept in the App’s settings. You can clear App storage through Android settings, and manage microphone and notification permissions and system backups through your device or provider settings. Clearing storage or uninstalling the App does not itself request deletion of information already sent to Google.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing of, object to processing of, or receive a copy of your personal information. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, contact airoun.team@gmail.com. We do not maintain a mapping between support email addresses and Analytics or Crashlytics installation identifiers. An email address alone therefore does not allow us to locate a particular installation’s records. Because recordings and sleep records stay on your device, we cannot access, export, or delete them for you; you can remove them using the controls above. We will explain any identification limitations and assess requests under applicable law; this does not remove your statutory rights. We will not ask you to create an App account to submit a privacy request.
